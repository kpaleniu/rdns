### 176. `rdnsr` has no negative trust anchors (RFC 7646) — **filed 2026-10-05**, **needs-triage**

When somebody else's zone breaks its DNSSEC, the only remedies are turning
validation off globally (`dnssec_validate`, `rdnsr/src/config.rs:107`) or the
client setting CD. RFC 7646 is the per-domain alternative; Unbound has
`domain-insecure` and `unbound-control insecure_add`, PowerDNS Recursor
`rec_control add-nta`, BIND `rndc nta`.

Searched, nothing found: `negative.?trust`, `insecure_domain`,
`domain_insecure`, `NegativeTrust`, `validate_except` in code; `7646`,
`negative trust`, `domain-insecure` in docs. Not a recorded decision.

A runtime NTA wants a control channel `rdnsr` does not have (#178); a
config-file one does not.
