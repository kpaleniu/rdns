### 174. `rdnsr` has no local data except through RPZ — **filed 2026-10-05**, **needs-triage**

Unbound has `local-zone`/`local-data`, BIND serves zones from a resolving
view, PowerDNS Recursor has `auth-zones`. Searched, nothing found:
`local.?zone`, `local.?data`, `static.?zone`, `hosts.?file`, `auth.?zone` in
`rdnsr/src` and `docs/spec/05`, `06`.

The nearest thing is RPZ's local-data action (`Action::LocalData`,
`rdns/src/rpz.rs:74`, read at `:695-727`), which answers any RRset of the
queried type at the trigger. Limits, each checked:

- the owner is rewritten to the QNAME (`rpz.rs:730`);
- AD is clear and EDE 4 (Forged Answer) is attached (`docs/spec/05` §5.6) —
  correct for a policy rewrite, wrong for data the operator owns;
- a CNAME at the trigger is always the action;
- per-name overrides, not a zone: nothing is authoritative or signable;
- it cannot reach anything `special_names` answers first (#173).

`special_names.rs` is a fixed table and not configurable.
