### 181. `rdnsd` gives every client the same answer: no views, no geo — **filed 2026-10-05**, **needs-triage**

`write_response(msg, zones, metrics, max_len, advertised, scratch)`
(`rdnsd/src/answer.rs:36-43`) takes no peer and no key, so the answer cannot
depend on who asked. Searched, nothing found: `split.horizon`, `\bviews?\b`
(type sense only), `allow.query`, `match.clients`, `geoip`, `\blua\b`. ECS is
declined on purpose (`docs/spec/07`, RFC 7871 row), which rules out the
resolver-side half of geo answers. Not a recorded decision.

Peers: BIND views and `geoip` ACLs; Knot `mod-geoip`; PowerDNS LUA records,
the GeoIP backend, and views since 5.0.0 (experimental, LMDB only).

Filed so the absence has a number. Whether it is in scope is the triage
question; a `wontfix` with the reason belongs here if not.
