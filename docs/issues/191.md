### 191. `rdnsr` emits no dnstap — **filed 2026-10-07**, **needs-triage**

Found triaging #189, which wanted somewhere machine-readable to record an
upstream's NSID and found only the log.

dnstap is `rdnsd`'s alone: `rdnsd/src/dnstap.rs`, and nothing under
`rdnsr/src` names it. `rdns::dnstap::MessageType` holds `AuthQuery`,
`AuthResponse`, `UpdateQuery` and `UpdateResponse`; its doc comment says the
resolver-side types "exist in the schema and are not here", because nothing
emits them. Searched every closed item that mentions dnstap — thirteen, plus
`docs/closed/log.md` — for a decision that the resolver should go without:
none makes one.

The schema's resolver-side types are `CLIENT_QUERY`/`CLIENT_RESPONSE` (what a
stub asked and was told) and `RESOLVER_QUERY`/`RESOLVER_RESPONSE` and
`FORWARDER_QUERY`/`FORWARDER_RESPONSE` (what this resolver asked upstream).

No remedy filed: which of those to emit, and whether the existing writer in
`rdns::dnstap` serves both daemons unchanged, is triage's call.
