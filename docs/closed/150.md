### 150. The authoritative answer is split across the `rdns`/`rdnsd` seam — **filed 2026-10-01, closed 2026-10-02**, **enhancement**, **ready-for-agent**

`rdns::dnssec_answer` has one production caller, `rdnsd/src/answer.rs`, and
its interface hands that caller the RFC 4035 §3.1 table to apply:

- five `pub fn` (`is_signed` and four `push_*`), and the caller picks which
  proof each outcome owes — `add_answer`, `add_negative`, `refer_to_child`;
- an obligation returned as a `bool`: `push_answer_signatures` is true when
  the answer "still owes `push_proof_of_absence`", which `rdnsd` discharges
  through `target_owed` (`answer.rs:238`), a `u32` bitmask (`:568`) and
  `add_chain_denials` (`:606`);
- an invariant whose guarantor is on the other side: #79e found
  `dnssec_answer.rs:200`'s claim holds because `resolve_in_zone` asks
  `delegation_for` first.

Because the algorithm cannot be reached from `rdns`, three places there
re-enact it by hand. `tests/allocations.rs`:
`answering_a_signed_query` ("As `answer.rs` does it: locate once…") and
`the_lookups_behind_one_answer_allocate_only_the_answer` ("the three zone
lookups `rdnsd` makes per query"). `tests/no_input_panics.rs` calls the four
`push_*` one by one. ~~`benches/answer_path.rs`'s `answer` group serializes a
built `DnsMessage`, which `rdnsd` stopped doing at #27b.~~ **Narrowed
2026-10-01, in grilling:** only its "one whole answer" bench is a stand-in —
it clones a parsed `DnsMessage`, fills `answers` and serializes, which is
neither `rdnsd`'s streaming path (#27b) nor `rdnsr`'s cache path. The
serialize-only benches beside it are `rdnsr`'s path as it stands, which still
builds a `DnsMessage` and calls `to_bytes_within`. That bench is what the
522 ns under "Where to pick up next" quotes. None of the three
follows `rdnsd` if its sequence changes.

Refuting check taken: the zone half of `answer.rs` — `Hop`, `Outcome`,
`resolve_in_zone`, `redirect_through`, `in_zone`, the `add_*` helpers,
`negative_ttl`, `refer_to_child` (`:270`-`:741`) — names only `rdns` types.
`Zones` and `Scratch` are used above `:270` only, and the only item any
other `rdnsd` module imports from `answer.rs` is `write_response`.

What it does not buy: `answer.rs`'s 44 tests go through `make_response`,
which needs `Zones`, `Cli` and `generate_keys`, so they stay in `rdnsd`.
What moves is what `rdns`'s counts, panic sweep and bench measure: the real
sequence rather than a copy. A move would have to hold every count in
`rdns/tests/allocations.rs` (§17). No remedy costed.

#### Agent brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Move RFC 1034 §4.3.2 for one zone, with the DNSSEC records it
owes, into one `rdns` module behind one function.

**Current behavior:** `rdnsd`'s answer module chooses the zone and then walks
RFC 1034 §4.3.2 itself (`resolve_in_zone`, `Outcome`, `Hop`,
`MAX_REDIRECTS`, the `add_*` helpers, `negative_ttl`, `refer_to_child`),
calling `rdns::dnssec_answer`'s `is_signed` and four `push_*` functions and
carrying their wildcard-denial obligation as a `bool` and a `u32` bitmask.
`rdns`'s allocation tests, panic sweep and "one whole answer" bench each
re-enact that sequence by hand.

**Desired behavior:** a module `rdns::zone_answer` whose only public item is

```rust
pub fn write(
    zone: &Zone,
    qname: &Name,     // as asked; the case is echoed (RFC 1034 §4.3.3)
    qtype: Qtype,
    dnssec_ok: bool,
    w: &mut ResponseWriter,
) -> Result<(), WireError>
```

It writes the answer, authority and additional sections for one question
against one already-chosen zone, sets NXDOMAIN or YXDOMAIN, clears AA on a
referral, and adds every DNSSEC record the reply owes when `dnssec_ok` and
the zone is signed. `rdnsd` keeps everything about the request rather than
the zone: REFUSED for an unserved class, FORMERR for AXFR over UDP, the
IXFR-over-UDP SOA, `Zones::for_query` and REFUSED with `NOT_OUR_ZONE`, the
header, the opcode, and the EDNS mirroring at the end. Nothing on the wire
changes.

**Key interfaces:**
- `rdns::dnssec_answer` becomes a private part of `zone_answer`;
  `is_signed`, `push_answer_signatures`, `push_proof_of_absence`,
  `push_negative_proof` and `push_delegation_proof` stop being `pub`.
- `Outcome`, `Hop` and `MAX_REDIRECTS` are private to the module;
  `resolve_in_zone` stays testable from the module's own tests.
- `rdnsd`'s `write_response` keeps its signature and calls
  `zone_answer::write` after selecting the zone.
- The module's `//!` header says why the split is here — the library holds
  RFC 1034 §4.3.2 and its proofs, the daemon holds zone selection, which is
  server policy (catalog, replication, withdrawal) — and uses the
  **Zone answer** term.
- #79e's invariant (`*.<closest encloser>` absent, guaranteed by the
  referral case being tried first) names its guarantor inside the module.
