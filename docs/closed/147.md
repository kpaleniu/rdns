### 147. A zone-file field past a name's RDATA was joined into the name — **filed and closed 2026-09-28**, **bug**

Found probing 145e. `rdata_from_fields` read NS, CNAME, PTR and DNAME from
`rdata`, the fields joined by a space, and MX joined every field after the
preference. A stray field became a label with a space in it:

| line | loaded as |
|---|---|
| `@ IN NS ns1.example.com. junk` | `ns1.example.com.\032junk.example.com.` |
| `x IN CNAME a.example.com. junk` | `a.example.com.\032junk.example.com.` |
| `@ IN MX 10 mail.example.com. junk` | `mail.example.com.\032junk.example.com.` |
| `@ IN SOA ns. h. 1 2 3 4 5 6` | the eighth field dropped unread |
| `@ IN SOA "ns one.example.com." h. 1 2 3 4 5` | refused, where CNAME took the same name |

A `<domain-name>` in RDATA is one field (RFC 1035 §3.3). A and AAAA read the
joined text too, and refuse a second field because it does not parse as an
address.

**Closed 2026-09-28**, `a5ea7e8`. `one_name()` takes exactly one field for
the four name types; MX takes exactly two and SOA exactly seven, both through
`fields`. Two tests, both failing against the old parser. Every tracked
interop zone still parses except `big.test.db`, which is `$GENERATE` (#21).
