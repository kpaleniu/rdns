### 138. An NSEC3 salt over 255 octets is encoded with the wrong length — **filed and closed 2026-09-28**, **bug**

Found by the idiom review of 2026-09-28. `ParsedRecord::encode`'s NSEC3 arm
writes `salt.len() as u8` and `next_hashed_owner.len() as u8`
(`rdns-core/src/record.rs:610`, `:612`). The zone-file parser hex-decodes the
salt and base32hex-decodes the next owner with no bound
(`rdns/src/zone/rdata.rs`, NSEC3 arm), and `RecordData::from_parsed` does not
re-parse what `encode` produced.

Provoked through `parse_zone_file` with a 256-octet salt: accepted, RDLENGTH
285, salt-length octet **0**, and the stored RDATA *parses* — as a different
NSEC3, salt empty and the hash length read from the salt's first octet. A
wrong record, served, nothing refused. The signer's own path is not affected:
`zone_signer.rs:105` refuses a salt over 255 before it gets here.

The doc comment on `RdataArena::push_parsed` (`record_data.rs:~358`) says
`encode` is `decode`'s inverse. It is not for this input (§4).

Swept: 14 `len() as u8` / `len() as u16` in production code. The other 12
are bounded before the cast — label and name limits (`dname.rs:221`,
`name.rs:171`, `:372`), RDLENGTH (`record_data.rs:351`), a bitmap window of
at most 32 octets (`denial_wire.rs:202`), `dnssec_key.rs:841` under its own
`<= 255`, `zone_signer.rs:1249` behind `:105`, and five in `tsig.rs` whose
fields are a MAC or were read from a u16 length.
