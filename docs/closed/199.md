## Triage decisions (2026-10-09)

- The caps stay. `//`: two lines. `///`: one paragraph of at most eight
  lines, or a summary of at most two and one paragraph of at most six.
  Three-line `//` runs are in scope.
- Exempt: `//!` module docs; fenced code and table rows, which do not count.
- An RFC quote is cut to the clause that carries the claim; the section
  number stays.
- A cut design argument that is hard to reverse, surprising and a real
  trade-off becomes an ADR, linked from the comment; anything else goes to
  the commit message.
- Test code is in scope, as each crate's last commit.
- The limits and the script landed in `CLAUDE.md` "Writing comments" before
  the sweep.
- Done is not zero: each block left over the cap is listed with its reason
  in the closing note.
